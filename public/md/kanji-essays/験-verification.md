# 験 (testing, experience, sign)

## What this kanji is, at its core

験 is the kanji for putting something to the test. It captures the moment you try something out to see what happens — an experiment, an exam, or simply the act of finding out. It also carries the idea of a sign or result that comes from that testing: the evidence that tells you it's real or it works.

## Common words that carry the 験's soul

### 試験 (しけん, examination / test)

- This is the most common word for a formal test, especially in school.
- Everyday use: 試験に合格した (しけんにごうかくした, I passed the exam)

### 実験 (じっけん, experiment)

- This is the scientific side of 験: testing an idea to see if it’s true.
- Everyday use: 理科の実験をする (りかのじっけんをする, I do a science experiment)

### 経験 (けいけん, experience)

- Here 験 points to something you've personally gone through and tested in real life, not just read about.
- Everyday use: いい経験になりました (いいけいけんになりました, It was a good experience)

### 体験 (たいけん, personal experience / hands-on experience)

- Like 経験 but more personal — something you directly lived through with your own body and senses.
- Everyday use: いろいろな体験をしたい (いろいろなたいけんをしたい, I want to have various experiences)

## Takeaway

験 is the kanji of putting something to the real test — a horse proving itself, an experiment proving a theory, a charm proving it works. Whether it's an exam, a lab result, or a lucky ritual before a big day, 験 is always asking the same question: did it actually hold up?
